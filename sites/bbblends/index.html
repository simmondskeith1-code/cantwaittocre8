Our Story

A family dream, poured fresh

BB Blends is family owned and rooted in Keyport. What started as a love for real fruit and real juice became a juicery, a wellness bodega and a tropical market under one roof.

"I've always dreamed of creating a place of wellness that feels welcoming and accessible…"BB Blends founder

You'll also find products from local vendors on our shelves, like handmade soaps, because good neighbors help each other grow.
