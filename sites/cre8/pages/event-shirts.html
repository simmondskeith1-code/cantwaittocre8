Event Shirts questions

Can you put a photo on a shirt?

Yes. Full color printing handles photos. Send the clearest copy you have and we tell you if it will print sharp.

We need shirts by a set date. Can you do it?

Put your event date in your order. We confirm it fits before you pay anything. Orders of 5 pieces or fewer are done 3 days after you approve.

Can everyone order their own size?

Yes. Send one list of names and sizes, or collect them and send them together.
