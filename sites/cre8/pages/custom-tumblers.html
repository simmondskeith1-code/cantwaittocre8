Tumblers and Mugs questions

Can you engrave a tumbler I already own?

Often yes. Tell us the brand and material and we confirm it works on our machines before we start.

Do engraved tumblers fade in the dishwasher?

Engraving removes the coating, so there is no ink to wash off. Hand washing still keeps the finish looking newer for longer.

Can you do a set with different names?

Yes. List the names in your order and you see each one on your mockup.
