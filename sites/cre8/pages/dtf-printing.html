DTF Printing questions

What is DTF printing?

DTF stands for direct to film. Your design is printed on a special film with an adhesive powder, then pressed onto the garment with heat and pressure.

How is DTF different from screen printing?

Screen printing needs a screen per color, so it pays off on big runs of simple designs. DTF has no setup per color, so full color designs and small orders cost less.

How do I wash a DTF shirt?

Turn it inside out, wash cold and tumble dry low or hang dry. Skip bleach and do not iron directly on the print.
