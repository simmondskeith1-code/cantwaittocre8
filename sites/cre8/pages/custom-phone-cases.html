Custom Phone Cases questions

What is the difference between 2D and 3D cases?

A 2D case is a flat full color print. A 3D case builds the print up so parts of the design stand off the case and you can feel them.

Which phones do you make cases for?

Tell us your exact phone model when you order. We confirm we can get a case that fits before anything is made.

Can I order cases for a group?

Yes. List each phone model in your order and you see every case on your mockup.
