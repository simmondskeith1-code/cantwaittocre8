Embroidery questions

What do I need to send for embroidery?

Your logo in the best file you have. We tell you how it stitches best, including size and thread colors.

Can you embroider small text?

Very small text can blur in thread. We size it so it reads clearly and show you on the mockup.

Is there a minimum for embroidery?

No. One piece is fine.
