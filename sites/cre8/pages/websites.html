Website questions

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. Your domain, hosting and code live in accounts in your name. If you ever leave, you keep everything.

Do I need a care plan?

No, it is optional. Sites need security fixes, backups and small changes, and things outside your site change too, like email and domain settings. A plan means someone is responsible for keeping it working. Without one, the site is handed to you at launch and any later fixes or edits are billed at $85/hour with a 1 hour minimum. If you choose a plan, you can cancel with 30 days notice after the first 3 months.

What does hosting cost?

For most local business sites, $0 to $13 a month. A simple site with no admin tools hosts free. A site with lead forms and your own admin dashboard runs about $7 a month, and about $13 a month if it stores data like orders or bookings. Add your domain name, usually $12 to $20 a year. Hosting is billed to you directly by the hosting company, with no markup from us. Our time for upkeep is covered by your care plan.

How long does it take?

About 2 weeks for a Launch Site and 3 to 4 weeks for a Growth Site, counted from when we have your content: logo, photos and text, or the add-ons that cover them.

What if I need changes after launch?

Edits are included in every care plan up to its monthly hours. Bigger additions are quoted before any work starts.

Do you do SEO?

Yes. Every site we build launches search-ready: local business data matched to your Google profile, Google verification and page submission set up, service pages and a FAQ that answers real customer questions. After launch, the optional Local Search add-on (from $450/mo) handles your Google profile, reviews and new pages, with a Search Report every month. We never guarantee rankings, because no one honestly can.

Can you work with my existing GoDaddy or Wix site?

Yes. We move your domain over without touching your email, and we can bring your existing content with us.
