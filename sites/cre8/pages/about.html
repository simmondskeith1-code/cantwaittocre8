Built while learning to walk again.

Can't Wait to Cre8 started in recovery. After a spinal cord injury, I was relearning how to move, one step at a time. I needed something to pour my focus into while my body did the slow work of healing. So I started creating.

Get a Free Mockup Read the Story

Creation kept me moving

Recovery is a lot of waiting. Waiting for strength to come back, for balance to return, for the next small win. I wanted something that moved forward every day, even on the days my body did not.

Making things gave me that. A design on a screen became a shirt in my hands. A name became an engraving someone would keep for years. Every finished piece was proof that something new could still come out of a hard season.

That is where the name comes from. I could not wait to create. I still can't.

"I believe creation is the essence of what it means to be alive. I want to help people rediscover what it means to live a full life."Keith Simmonds Jr.
