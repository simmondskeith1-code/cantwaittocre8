Built while learning to walk again.

Can't Wait to Cre8 started in recovery. After surgery for severe spinal stenosis, I was relearning how to move, one step at a time. I needed something to pour my focus into while my body did the slow work of healing. So I started creating.

Creation kept me moving

Recovery is a lot of waiting. Waiting for strength to come back, for balance to return, for the next small win. I wanted something that moved forward every day, even on the days my body did not.

Making things gave me that. A design on a screen became a shirt in my hands. A name became an engraving someone would keep for years. Every finished piece was proof that something new could still come out of a hard season.

That is where the name comes from. I could not wait to create. I still can't.

"I believe creation is the essence of what it means to be alive. I want to help people rediscover what it means to live a full life."Keith Simmonds Jr.

From a walker to a print shop

  1. 2018

    Diagnosis and surgery

    Severe spinal stenosis and surgery meant starting over, including learning to walk again. Creating gave my mind something to build while my body rebuilt.

  2. 2020

    The first order

    The first paid order came in, and the shop was officially open for business.

  3. 2021

    Our own place

    Can't Wait to Cre8 opened at 37 Center Ave in Leonardo, NJ.

  4. 2025

    Laser engraving

    Our first laser engraving job, adding tumblers, wood, leather and gifts to what we make.

  5. Now

    Today

    Printing, embroidery, laser engraving and full color work, helping you make the things that matter to you.
