Rules of the Road

How we sell

Cre8 is a local shop. Every owner you talk to knows other owners. One pushy visit costs more than ten good ones earn.

📍
Claim your leads

Log every visit within 24 hours. The first rep to log a business owns it for 60 days. Keith's existing clients and leads are not claimable. No log, no commission dispute you can win.

🏷️
Check founding slots before quoting

Founding prices are real and limited. Look at the slots counter on this page before you quote. When it hits zero, you quote standard prices. No exceptions.

👕
Wear the product

We are a print shop. Wear your Cre8 shirt, keep it clean, carry business cards and ID. You are the walking sample.

⏰
Read the room

Never pitch during a rush. Restaurants: 2 to 4 PM. Salons and shops: first hour after opening. If there is a line, buy something small or leave a card and come back.

🚫
Never promise results

No "page one of Google," no "you'll get X new customers," no launch dates without checking Keith's build calendar. Promise the work, not the outcome.

💵
Never touch money

No cash, no personal Venmo, no card numbers. Deposits only through the invoice Cre8 sends from Stripe or Square.

🙊
Never trash their current web person

Their nephew might be in the back. Talk about what customers need, not about who failed them.

✋
No means not today

Respect "No Soliciting" signs. One clear no at the door, you thank them and leave a card. One follow-up visit maximum unless they invite you back. Some towns require a solicitor permit: Keith confirms yours before you start.
